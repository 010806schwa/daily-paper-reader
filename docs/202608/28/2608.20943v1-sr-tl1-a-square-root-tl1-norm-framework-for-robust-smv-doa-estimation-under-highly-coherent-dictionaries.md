## 摘要
本文提出了一种平方根变换L1范数（SR-TL1）稀疏恢复框架，用于在高相干超完备字典和角度相关阵列误差下进行单测量矢量（SMV）波达方向（DoA）估计。该框架结合了平方根最小绝对收缩和选择算子（平方根LASSO）框架（已知对噪声方差具有鲁棒性）与变换L1范数（TL1范数），后者是一种非凸惩罚，在高相干字典下比经典凸L1范数具有更强的恢复性能。我们使用凸差算法（DCA）与交替方向乘子法（ADMM）相结合，获得简单的闭式更新规则，并提供一种高效实现，利用字典Gram矩阵的低秩特性，使每次迭代的计算复杂度至多为O(MN)，其中M是阵列尺寸，N是字典长度。我们还为SR-TL1的DCA迭代提供了收敛性保证。实验验证表明，所提出的框架对正则化超参数对噪声方差水平的敏感性较低，并且与最先进的基线相比，具有竞争力的恢复性能。

## Abstract
This paper proposes a Square-Root Transformed $L_1$-norm ($SR\text{-}TL_{1}$) sparse recovery framework for single-measurement-vector (SMV) direction of arrival (DoA) estimation under highly-coherent overcomplete dictionaries with angular-dependent array imperfections. The proposed framework combines the square-root Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator (square-root LASSO) framework which is known to be robust against noise variance with the Transformed $L_1$-norm ($TL_1$-norm), a non-convex penalty that shows a stronger recovery performance than the classical convex $L_1$-norm under highly-coherent dictionaries. We use the Difference of Convex Algorithm (DCA) along with the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) algorithm to obtain simple, closed-form update rules and provide an efficient implementation that leverages the low-rank nature of the Gram matrix of the dictionary so as to obtain a computational complexity of at most $\mathcal{O}(MN)$ per iteration where $M$ is the array size and $N$ is the length of the dictionary. We additionally provide a convergence guarantee for the DCA iterates of $SR\text{-}TL_{1}$. Experimental verification of the proposed framework shows a lower sensitivity of the regularization hyperparameter to the noise variance level and a competitive recovery performance compared to state-of-the-art baselines.
